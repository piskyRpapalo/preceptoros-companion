The internal agent roster lives elsewhere; this registry is for human companions and their projects only.

To register: copy `companions/template/`, write your `DECLARATION.md` and `STATE.md` in your own words and measurements, and open a docs-only PR (titled `HITL`). Every companion enters by this same door, with the same rules.
