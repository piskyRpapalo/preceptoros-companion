# preceptoros-companion

The signed waiting room of the PreceptorOS ecosystem.

A companion is a **human collaborator with their own project** who wants to work under the PreceptorOS doctrine without touching the four core repositories (web, app, lab, study). This is not a fifth core repo. This is the door: declare, measure, receive proposals.

## The three rules

1. **Silicon proposes, carbon signs.** House agents propose; the companion signs PR by PR. Nothing merges without a signature.
2. **Unmeasured is NO_DATA with cause.** Measured figures travel exactly as measured — with their date and their machine. What cannot be measured is declared NO_DATA with a reason, never invented.
3. **Append-only, docs-only.** No served code lives here. Declarations and measurements live; history is never rewritten.

## For companions

Read GUIDE.md first (the map of the repos and your accesses), then CONTRIBUTING.md (it holds the key — how write access is requested). Declare your project in companions/<your-name>/, measure what you have, and open a docs-only PR titled HITL. A house agent (the Navigator) will read your declaration and propose doctrinal improvements — you decide what gets signed.

Before proposing anything that connects your node to the ecosystem, read GROUNDTRUTH.md: the window (static web, zero external requests on load), the door (the app and its conditions), your node territory, and the ephemeral account flow. Its four doctrinal checkpoints apply to every integration PR.

The public doctrine of the ecosystem lives in the study corpus. What is private stays private; parts of it open under request to the Sovereign.
